Your Austin Advisor

Real estate insight built on real experience.

“My mission is simple: to help you avoid costly pitfalls and unnecessary frustration while guiding you home — at the right time and the right price.”

Since 1997, I’ve built a hands-on foundation in real estate that goes far beyond the transaction. My experience spans carpentry, roofing, siding, drywall, painting, flipping, remodeling, property management, project management, data analysis, lending, and brokerage. That depth lets me spot opportunities — and risks — that others often miss.

Before real estate, my career spanned the entertainment industry and corporate leadership — from Broadway and studios like Disney, Nickelodeon, HBO, and Universal, to a decade with Fortune 100 & 500 companies in sales, training, and regional management.

I take my fiduciary responsibility seriously. Your best interests are always my top priority — no exceptions.

How It Works

A clear path, every step of the way.

Buying

  1. Get pre-approved with a trusted lender to understand your budget.
  2. Define your search — location, schools, commute, and future development.
  3. Tour properties with a strategic eye for condition and true value.
  4. Make an offer that is competitive and well structured.
  5. Inspections & due diligence with repair estimates to negotiate credits.
  6. Close & celebrate with title and escrow coordinated for you.

Selling

  1. Accurate pricing from comparable sales and neighborhood data.
  2. Staging & preparation — simple, high-impact improvements.
  3. Professional marketing with photography and compelling copy.
  4. Online & offline visibility through MLS, portals, and agent outreach.
  5. Negotiation & offer management to maximize your outcome.
  6. A smooth close — on time and on terms that work for you.

Services & Fees

Transparent, no surprises.

You deserve to know exactly what you’re paying for, and why, before you sign anything. Commission is negotiable, and there are no hidden fees.

When Douglas represents the seller

Selling a property
4–6% total commission, negotiable, paid at closing
Leasing a property
90% of one month’s rent, split with cooperating agent

When Douglas represents the buyer or tenant

New construction
3%, typically paid by the builder
Resale
3%, most often paid by the seller
Leasing
Landlord-compensated; tenant rebate may apply

$150 per hour
